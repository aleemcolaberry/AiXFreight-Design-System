Trust ≠ confidence
Trust · the system
“Do I believe the copilot is reliable for reroutes?”
Confidence · this answer
“Do I have enough evidence to switch lane 2 to air now?”
An operator can trust the copilot in general yet hesitate on one call because the sources are thin. Measure both; fix confidence with evidence (AiDisclosure), not with bolder styling.
