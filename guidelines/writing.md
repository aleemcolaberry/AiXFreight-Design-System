# Writing

Voice: calm operator. Confident, specific, quantified. Second person for the user, "we" only in reassurance.

- Headings: sentence case in source, uppercase via CSS (Saira Condensed). Buttons: verb + object, sentence case. Labels: mono caps nouns.
- Numbers beat adjectives: "−4.1 days · +$1,120 · 94%". Money and IDs in mono. Separate facts with " · ", lanes with " → ".
- Chunk IDs: AIX-48211, MSKU 482 119-0.
- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no "successfully", no anthropomorphized AI ("the AI recommends", never "I think").
- Errors: what happened + how to fix, no blame. Empty states: display title + one-line fix + one action.
- Toasts: past-tense verb + object, offer Undo. Dialogs: title is the question ("Reroute to air?"), primary button repeats the verb ("Apply").
